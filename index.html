Who is Shadid?

He was never satisfied with being only one thing. Architecture student at BRAC University, yet architecture was only one part of him. He drew, painted, built models, designed websites, experimented with code, imagined games, read deeply, and constantly tried to turn the ideas in his head into something real.

He carried an unusual combination of an artist’s sensitivity and an engineer’s curiosity. He could care about the tiniest detail of a line while also wondering how to build an entire digital world from scratch. There were disappointments, pressure, family complications, and moments when he questioned where his life was going. Yet one pattern kept returning: he kept trying to build something.

He is proud of where he came from — his science background, Golden A+ in both SSC and HSC, 8th merit position in BRAC Architecture admission, drawing awards, and the handmade portfolio book that itself became a work of art. At the same time he is honestly exploring a transition toward Computer Science & Engineering. He imagines things larger than his current abilities. Sometimes that makes projects messy or unfinished. It also means he is always reaching beyond what he already knows.

If one sentence could hold him: Shadid is someone still becoming — turning imagination into reality through lines, buildings, colours, code, stories and ideas.

The Portfolio Book

This portfolio is entirely handmade — from drawings and models to every cut, composition and binding. Binding, colour coding, logo design, fonts, and even the three-dimensional cut-out models stacked inside were crafted by hand. It is both a 2-D document and a 3-D object. The scanned pages and physical models live together in one volume that itself forms part of the creative process.
