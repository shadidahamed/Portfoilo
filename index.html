Who He Was Becoming

A personal reflection

Profile

He was never satisfied with being only one thing. An architecture student, yes — but architecture was only one part of him. He drew, painted, built models, designed websites, experimented with code, imagined games, explored technology, read books, and constantly tried to turn the ideas in his head into something real.

He carried a strange combination of an artist’s sensitivity and an engineer’s curiosity. He could care about the tiniest detail of a drawing while also wondering how to build an entire digital world from scratch. He loved learning things he did not yet know how to do. Sometimes that made his projects messy, ambitious or unfinished. But it also meant he was always reaching for something beyond what he already knew.

There were difficulties, pressure, and moments when the path felt unclear. Yet one thing kept appearing again and again: he kept trying to build something. He wanted his work to mean something — not simply to look impressive, but to tell people who he was.

If one sentence could hold him, it would be this: Shadid was a person who kept trying to turn imagination into reality — through lines, buildings, colours, code, stories and ideas. He was not someone who had everything figured out. He was someone who was still becoming.

The Book

A completely handmade portfolio

This portfolio is entirely handmade — from its drawings and models to every cut, composition and binding. Each element was crafted by hand, making the portfolio itself a part of the creative process. Binding, colour coding, logo design, lettering, and the attached physical models (Swiss board cut-outs stacked into three-dimensional forms) were all created by Shadid. It is both a 2D document and a 3D object.
