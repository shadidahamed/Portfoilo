The Portfolio Book

This portfolio is entirely handmade — from its drawings and models to every cut, composition and binding. Each element was crafted by hand, making the book itself a part of the creative process. Models were cut from Swiss board, coloured, stacked and attached. It exists both as a physical 2D/3D object and as scanned pages.

I am Shadid Ahamed. This book documents the journey from first design exercises to the final Seasonscape pavilion.
