“A person who keeps trying to turn imagination into reality.”

He was never satisfied with being only one thing. Across drawing, painting, architecture, model-making, web design, code, digital experiments, literature, music and visual storytelling, he moved with the sensitivity of an artist and the curiosity of an engineer.

The path was not smooth. There were disappointments, pressure, uncertainty and difficult periods. Yet repeatedly he returned to creating, learning and rebuilding — still becoming, still exploring the intersection of Architecture, Design, Art and Computer Science.

Imagination is not enough; it must become form, space, line, and living digital presence. That is the quiet ambition that continues to shape his work.

The Book

Handmade Portfolio · Physical Creative Process

This portfolio is entirely handmade — from its drawings and models to every cut, composition and binding. Each element was crafted by hand, making the portfolio itself a part of the creative process. Binding, colour coding, typography, logo work, cutouts, layered forms and architectural model fragments were all shaped through the author’s own hands. The book is not merely a container; it is the work.

Architectural Models

From Scratch to Final · ARC 101 · Summer 2026 · Roll 100061008

Each model begins with a question of space, order and material. From the first grid of black squares to the monsoon pavilion, the work moves through visual order, lines found in nature, low-relief carving, planes in composition, and the subtraction of volumes — ending in a spatial interpretation of seasonal rhythm.
