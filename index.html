ARTWORK 01

Flower Grows in Silence

—

A figure partly underwater surrounded by lotus, green leaves, fish and distant land, exploring confinement, separation, passage of time and silent growth of beauty.

INTERPRETATION

Water texture represents confinement. A flower growing from within represents silent thought, beauty and the possibility of freedom. Fish represent the flow of time, while distant land separates the figure from worldly life.
