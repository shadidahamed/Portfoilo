Profile

A mind shaped by space,
now learning to shape systems.

I grew up measuring the world with a pencil and a T-square. Architecture taught me to see structure, light, proportion, and the quiet dignity of materials. Every drawing was a decision; every model a hypothesis about how people might inhabit a place.

Somewhere along the way the questions changed. I began asking not only how a space feels, but how information moves, how interfaces breathe, how code can hold intention as carefully as concrete holds a floor. The transition from Architecture to Computer Science & Engineering is not a departure — it is a continuation of the same craft, expressed in a new medium.

I am a student from Bangladesh, carrying the discipline of the studio into the logic of the machine. I still sketch. I still build by hand. And now I also write systems that can think, respond, and endure.
