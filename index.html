01 — Profile

Who he is

Architecture student at BRAC University (ARC-101, Summer 2026). Visual thinker, maker, and digital experimenter. The work sits at the intersection of handcraft, geometric structure, and computational curiosity.

This is not a service catalogue. It is a record of how ideas are constructed — how materials become form, how visual language becomes interface, and how a student keeps building through uncertainty.

Dual interest: architecture as spatial thinking + computing as systemic thinking. The handmade portfolio book is the clearest evidence of that approach.

07 — Process / Persistence

How he keeps building

Some things did not happen the way expected. Some decisions were his. Some outcomes were outside his control. He learned from the work, the mistakes and the setbacks, and kept building.

Architecture. Drawing. Web. Games. Craft. Learning. The real record is not only the results — it is the decision to continue constructing after closed doors.

If I had to tell someone who Shadid was, I would not begin with his results or the things he failed to get. I would begin with how hard he kept trying.

He wanted a future badly enough to keep reaching for it even when life put obstacles in front of him. He worked toward architecture, pushed through admissions, studied when exhausted, practiced drawing, built portfolios, learned new things, and looked for another door whenever one closed.

There were many closed doors. Disappointments that hurt more than he usually let people see. Pressure from family, money, education, relationships and personal loss. Moments of being completely worn down. But he kept going.

He fought through academic pressure, disappointment, loneliness, financial stress, heartbreak — and still created. He drew, designed, imagined spaces, furniture, websites, portfolios, games and countless small ideas. He cared about details because making something beautiful and meaningful mattered.

The numbers do not tell the whole story. The real achievement was everything he had to overcome to reach them. Behind every drawing was patience, behind every plan was hope, behind every “I’ll try again” was someone who had already been through a lot.

His story was never just about whether he got into one university or whether one dream happened on schedule. It was about a person who kept reaching forward.
