01 · Profile

Who is speaking.

Architecture as disciplined curiosity.

I come from a science background and chose architecture not as an escape from rigor, but as a way to give rigor a spatial form. Drawing, making, and thinking in sequence have been constant methods since school.

The handmade book for ARC-101 is not a portfolio exercise for me — it is a document of how I learn: by hand, by density of line, by returning to the same problem until it holds.

I am interested in the quiet between geometry and material, in models that carry light, and in the persistence required to finish something carefully when no one is watching.

03 · Process & Persistence

If I had to tell someone who Shadid was.

If I had to tell someone who Shadid was, I would not start with grades or titles. I would start with the hours spent at a desk when the work still refused to settle — the quiet decision to stay with a line, a joint, a composition until it held.

Persistence is not a slogan. It is the method by which a handmade book becomes more than paper, and a model becomes more than foam.

My father, Tofail Ahmed, is a Police Inspector. My mother has held the household with steady care. I have a wife and a sister. The family is ordinary in the best sense: work is expected, dignity is expected, and finishing what you start is not optional.

I carry that into the studio. Architecture, for me, is not performance. It is a long conversation with material, geometry, and the people who will one day inhabit the spaces we propose. I am still early in that conversation. I intend to stay in it.

Lage Raho.
