01 / Profile

A student between space and systems.

Currently studying Architecture at BRAC University, first semester, ARC-101, Summer 2026. Alongside architecture, Shadid is exploring a transition toward Computer Science and Engineering while continuing to work in drawing, craft, websites, games and visual communication.

The portfolio keeps architecture at the center while documenting a parallel digital direction.

The physical portfolio book is fully handmade: black mount / Swiss board, hand lettering, hatching, geometric composition, checker/grid construction and manual assembly. It is treated here not as a decorative object, but as evidence of process and design thinking.

The digital portfolio extends that language into a black spatial interface with grids, voids, subtle particles, restrained burgundy accents and interactive project viewers.

10 / Research & Ideas

Learning through becoming.

This page is intentionally reflective: it connects making, failure, experimentation and the long process of becoming better at the work.

Personal context is kept dignified and focused on learning, agency and output.

Persistence

Not every academic or personal outcome has followed the expected route. The useful response has been to keep learning, making, correcting and moving.

Self-correction

Some problems are created by our own choices. They can also become sources of knowledge when examined honestly rather than hidden.

Space

There is a recurring fascination with space, height, flight, the moon landing and the human urge to go further — balanced here with an acceptance that growth requires sustained work.

Architecture × Computing

The current interest is not to erase architecture. It is to see how spatial thinking, visual logic and programming can strengthen one another.

The aim is not to look finished. It is to become harder to stop.
