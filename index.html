ART 01 OPEN DETAIL

FEATURED WORK

Flower Grows in Silence

ART 01 MEDIUM — EDITABLE

A figure partly underwater, surrounded by lotus, green leaves and fish, with distant land beyond. Green clothing represents the underwater space, while yellow represents the world above water.

INTERPRETATION

Water texture represents confinement. The flower growing from inside represents silent thought, beauty and freedom. Fish represent the flow of time. The distant land represents separation from worldly life.
