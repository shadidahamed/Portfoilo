SHADIDAHAMED

Architecture · Creative Developer · Designer · Artist

He was never satisfied with being only one thing. Across drawing, painting, architecture, model-making, web design, code, digital experiments, literature, music and visual storytelling, he has kept returning to the same impulse: to give form to what is imagined.

He combines the sensitivity of an artist with the curiosity of an engineer. The path has not been perfectly smooth — there were disappointments, pressure, uncertainty and difficult periods. Yet repeatedly he returned to creating, learning and rebuilding.

He is not presented as someone who already has everything figured out. He is still becoming.

A person who keeps trying to turn imagination into reality.

Exploring the intersection of Architecture, Design, Art and Computer Science.

The Book

Handmade Portfolio

This portfolio is entirely handmade, from its drawings and models to every cut, composition and binding. Each element was crafted by hand, making the portfolio itself a part of the creative process.

Its binding, colour coding, typography, logo work, cutouts, layered forms, architectural model fragments, compositions and page construction were created as part of the author’s own creative process. The book is not simply a container for the work — the book itself is part of the work.
