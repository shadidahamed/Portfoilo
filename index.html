A NOTE ABOUT SHADID

If I had to tell someone who Shadid was, I would not begin with his results, his marks, or the things he failed to get. I would begin with how hard he kept trying.

He kept reaching for a future he could believe in — through admissions, study, drawing, architecture, personal projects, money pressure, disappointments and the many moments when another door seemed to close.

He was never defined only by success. The deeper story is that after being hurt or exhausted, he kept creating, kept learning and kept looking for another way forward.
